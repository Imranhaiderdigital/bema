Questions we hear often

Do you ship outside Florida?

Yes. We deliver nationwide with insured shipping. Give us your city or ZIP in the quote request and we will include delivery.

Can I see the stone in person?

Our showroom and warehouse is at 4900 W Rio Vista Ave in Tampa. Designer service is free there and over the phone.

Which stone should I choose for a pool deck?

Travertine and marble pavers are our main pool deck lines, and porcelain is a low-maintenance alternative. Finish, colour, sun exposure and sealing all matter, so talk it through with a designer before you decide.

Can you make custom bullnose?

Yes. We fabricate bullnose in our own facility on 1.25" and 2" thick travertine and marble pavers, including double and triple sided pieces for steps, corners and spa edges.

Do you make stone drains?

Yes, in travertine and marble. The standard size is 6x12, and custom sizes are available.

How much extra should I order?

Most projects order about 10% more than the measured area to cover cuts and breakage. Use the estimator above, and your account specialist will check the final number.

How do I get pricing?

Price depends on the stone, finish and quantity. Add products to your quote list and send the request, or call the product information line.
